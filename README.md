Materials:
	1 sheet 8.5" x 8.5" orange cardstock (I used one sheet of white 8.5" x 11" cardstock, and printed a borderless orange sheet)
  	1 sheet 8.5" x 8.5" white cardstock (I used one sheet of white 8.5" x 11" cardstock)
  	1 sheet 9.5" x 9.5" blue cardstock  (I used two sheets of white 8.5" x 11" cardstock, and printed a borderless blue sheet)
  	2 sheets of 9.5" x 9.5" cardboard (slighty larger might be easier to cut)
  	1 glue stick

If you don't have orange or blue cardstock, you can print these on white cardstock:
	1 - print - orange - color.svg
  	2 - print - blue - color.svg
  	(For blue, I only had 8.5" x 11", so I just printed out two sheets and used that)
  	(if you don't have cardstock, plain paper would work, too)

Laser cut the orange paper:
  	3 - laser cut - orange.svg

Glue orange square to top of cap.

Laser cut the white paper, and one sheet of cardboard:
	4 - laser cut - white.svg

Glue white paper pieces onto cardboard pieces.

Glue white pieces onto cap.

Laser cut blue paper, and one sheet of cardboard:
	5 - laser cut - blue.svg

Glue blue paper pieces onto cardboard pieces.

Glue blue pieces onto cap.

Done!
</pre>
